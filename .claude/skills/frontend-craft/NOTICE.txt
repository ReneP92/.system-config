This is a locally maintained adaptation, not an upstream installation.
The reference revisions identify the sources reviewed, not an installed runtime.

Source mapping at those revisions:
  references/taste.md     -> taste-skill-classics skills/taste-skill-v1/SKILL.md,
                             skills/taste-skill-v2-experimental/SKILL.md and
                             taste-skill skills/taste-skill/references/
                             (system.md, layout-v4.md, motion.md, copy.md,
                             copy-tells.md)
  references/libraries.md -> emilkowalski/skills skills/pick-ui-library/SKILL.md
                             and ui-cheatsheet.md
  SKILL.md                -> the design read, dials and pick-ui-library method,
                             combined with this collection's workflow

Local adaptations keep the design read, the three dials, the anti-default rules
and the curated library method. They drop taste-skill's scripts, finish gate,
stop hook, randomly dealt palettes and layouts, and its landing-page-only rules,
and they defer to a product's existing design system. Library picks default to
shadcn/ui on Radix and add effect registries under explicit limits.

Maintenance: review upstream changes selectively, update the authored files and
these reference revisions together, and preserve this notice and the licenses
below when redistributing.
